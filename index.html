Welcome to Tabletop Voice

Tabletop Voice is an independent companion app. It does not bundle paid rulebooks, codex text or game artwork. Game data is shown only after you choose to load it from your own roster files or a supported game service.

Unofficial: Tabletop Voice is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Games Workshop, OnePageRules or other game publishers. Warhammer and related names and marks belong to their respective owners. Always use the publisher's current rules as the authoritative source.

This is a free public beta, so features, layouts and data handling may change while the app is being tested.
