Welcome to Tabletop Voice

Tabletop Voice is an independent companion app. It does not ship with Warhammer 40,000 rules, datasheets, codex text or artwork. Game content is shown only after you choose to load it from your own roster files or supported community-maintained web data.

Unofficial: Tabletop Voice is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Games Workshop. Warhammer and related names and marks belong to their respective owners. Always use official publications for authoritative rules, and only import data you are permitted to use.

This is a free public beta, so features, layouts and data handling may change while the app is being tested.
